About Us

Our Mission

The Northwest Jersey Athletic Conference is comprised of 39 public and private high schools located in Morris, Sussex, and Warren counties in New Jersey. Our member schools are dedicated to promoting excellence in athletics and sportsmanship.

The conference strives to promote fair competition among member schools, provide opportunities for student-athletes to develop their skills, foster positive relationships between schools and communities, and maintain high standards of integrity in all athletic programs.

All NJAC member schools belong to the New Jersey State Interscholastic Athletic Association (NJSIAA) and compete in accordance with NJSIAA rules and regulations.
