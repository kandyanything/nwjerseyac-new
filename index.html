Welcome to the NJAC

The Northwest Jersey Athletic Conference is comprised of 39 public and private high schools located in Morris, Sussex, and Warren counties in New Jersey. Our member schools are dedicated to promoting excellence in athletics and sportsmanship.

All athletic schedules are now available through Arbiter Live, providing real-time updates on games, events, and schedule changes.

About Arbiter Live

Arbiter Live is the platform used by NJAC schools to share athletic schedules. Parents, students, and fans can:

  • View real-time game schedules and results
  • Subscribe to team updates and notifications
  • Access schedules on any device
  • Receive alerts for schedule changes or cancellations

Click on any school under Member Schools to access their Arbiter Live schedule.
